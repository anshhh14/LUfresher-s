Origin Story

Your Story Starts Here

Every senior on this campus was a fresher once — nervous, curious, and one night away from finding their people. Fresher's Night is that night: the one where strangers from forty different classrooms become one loud, unstoppable batch.

Expect music, games, performances, a few surprises, and the kind of energy that only happens when an entire university welcomes its newest members at once. Come find your crew.
